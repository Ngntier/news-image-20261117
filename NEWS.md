## 1. Ukraine hits the Kerch Bridge — road span collapses
Ukrainian drones and missiles struck the Kerch Bridge linking Russia to
occupied Crimea; Ukraine's General Staff said an arch and a span of the road
section collapsed, severing the road link, and traffic was halted. President
Zelenskyy called it the final phase of a weeks-long, seven-stage operation.
Sources: Associated Press (via Montana Public Radio, 17 Nov 2026),
The Irish Times, 17 Nov 2026.

## 2. Trump lands in Manila for ASEAN
President Donald Trump arrived in the Philippines on Tuesday to open a
three-day Asia trip: Quad talks with Australia, India and Japan, a Thai
trade-deal signing and a US–ASEAN special summit in Manila, before travelling
to Shenzhen for talks with China's Xi Jinping and Russia's Vladimir Putin.
Sources: Associated Press (via KRVS, 17 Nov 2026), BusinessWorld, 17 Nov 2026.

## 3. Tankers burn in the Strait of Hormuz
Iran's Revolutionary Guard navy and regular navy said tankers struck naval
mines and caught fire in and around the Strait of Hormuz; a fourth tanker was
reported hit on Tuesday. The claims could not be independently verified, and
the UK Maritime Trade Operations agency separately reported tanker attacks
in the strait. The image captions this as reported, not confirmed.
Sources: Press TV, 17 Nov 2026; India Shipping News, 17 Nov 2026;
Al Jazeera / Reuters reporting summarised by Algenom, 17 Nov 2026.

## 4. Vulcan fails — 40 Amazon Leo satellites lost
A United Launch Alliance Vulcan rocket failed about 10 minutes into flight
on 15 November, losing 40 Amazon Leo satellites; ULA said on Tuesday 17
November that early data suggests the first stage underperformed just after
liftoff. It was ULA's first total mission failure in its 20-year history.
Sources: GeekWire (updated 17 Nov 2026), Spaceflight Now via GeekWire.

## 5. JPMorgan software bug slows US banks
JPMorgan Chase blamed a software bug for Monday outages that slowed online
banking and Zelle payments across several major US banks; the bank said there
was no evidence it was cyber-related and that services returned to normal.
Source: Associated Press (via The Hindu BusinessLine, 17 Nov 2026).

## 6. Japan scrambles jets after suspected Russian airspace violation
Japan scrambled fighter jets and protested to Moscow after suspected Russian
aircraft entered its airspace near Okinoshima early on Tuesday.
Source: Just Security, "Early Edition: November 17, 2026".
